Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains how Dave Got It Wrong ("we", "us") handles information on this website and in the "Dave Poster" app that we use to publish our own videos to our own social media accounts.

What the Dave Poster app does

Dave Poster is an internal tool. It is used only by us to upload videos we made to accounts we own (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook). It is not offered to the public and does not let other people log in.

Information we process

  • Our own account data: when we connect our own TikTok, Meta or Google account, the platform gives us an access token and our own account ID. Tokens are stored encrypted and are used only to publish our videos and read basic statistics (views, likes) about our own posts.
  • No data about viewers: we do not collect, store or sell personal data of viewers or other users. We do not use the app to read other people's profiles, messages or contacts.
  • This website: is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and has no analytics or ads. GitHub may log technical data (such as IP addresses) for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Email: if you email us, we use your message only to reply.

Sharing

We do not share or sell personal data. Content we publish is sent only to the platform it is posted on.

Retention and deletion

Access tokens are kept only while the connection is needed and can be revoked at any time in the platform's settings. To ask about or delete any information, email davegotitwrong@gmail.com.

Children

Our content is general-audience and not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.

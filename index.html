Dave has a plan. Dave always has a plan. And Dave is usually wrong.

WHAT IF…? Dave's "brilliant" idea  →  THE MISTAKE  →  THERMAL VIEW what is really happening  →  THE FIX a simple, free fix you can do yourself.

This Christmas, Dave has 39 days to fix the house he broke himself before his daughter Kylie comes home for dinner. Follow along.
